# QA report, round 1, 16 November 2026

Tester: independent QA (model: claude-sonnet-5-5). Build under test: `npm run build` static export, served with `npx serve out -l 3101`. Browser: Playwright Chromium. All scripts are in `/home/user/personal-site/qa/*.mjs`.

## Verdict: FAIL (no blockers, 3 major defects, 18 minor)

The site is close and is genuinely art-directed. It fails this round because the downloadable resume still publishes the client's age (which the client asked to remove), the tablet MUN chapter has a large dead area, and the line crosses a label in the phone constellation (an explicit do-not). All three are small fixes.

## Design judgement

**Reads as premium and art-directed: yes, in most of it.** The hero (name on the rule, portrait, one line), the finance hairpin with its crosshair, the Night block (route and circuit are the best-drawn pieces on the site), the Law document with the meridian as its margin rule, the timeline (the pin is smooth and the hand-off to the meridian is exact) and the quiz constellation all earn the brief. Type is confident (Instrument Serif at large sizes, Geist Mono for data), the palette holds, and the whole thing never falls into cards, glass or gradients. Each interest does have its own visual treatment inside one system.

**Weaker stretches:**
- Academic profile, Leadership and Competitions are long runs of ruled tables. Competitions is about 4,300px and MUN about 4,300px; between the figures they read like a well-set resume, which is the corporate-resume look the brief warns against. They are consistent but flat next to the interest chapters.
- The empty placeholder block ("In Ahaan's words, to come" over three blank rules) recurs twelve times. By instruction they are visible placeholders, but the repetition makes the page feel unfinished and is the main reason the middle of the page feels sparse.
- The tablet MUN void (defect 2) and the Tennis court (17) are the two compositional soft spots.

**The line:** continuous from the hero rule to the dot under the email at 1440, 834 and 390; the meridian sits at x=210, 40 and 20; the colour changes exactly at the Night edges (checked at entry and exit); every figure meets the meridian at entry and exit; the timeline's exit stub lands on the meridian. I found no break. It does cross text at the phone constellation label (defect 3) and transiently in the entrance (defect 10).

**Do-not list:** no cards, shadows, gradients, rounded corners, scroll cue, preloader, cursor follower, ninth figure or capitals found. The logos are in table logo columns or the timeline panel only. The two deviations are the orange text (defect 5) and a placeholder-heavy rhythm.

## Not tested, and why

- Touch behaviour of the globe (scroll-linked turn), the finance crosshair by tap, the compass tape by scroll and the circuit lap by tap: I used emulated touch only for screenshots and the phone timeline; I did not drive these touch paths.
- Smoothness and feel of Lenis, parallax, and the Night wipe and placard slide: Playwright screenshots do not show frame pacing; only end states were checked.
- Clicking a timeline entry to scroll it to the mark: my click attempt targeted an off-screen entry and timed out, so I cannot say it works; arrow keys, Home and End do.
- 200% zoom and the 320px "one word per line" name wrapping: I checked 320 for overflow only (none) and did not look at the layout.
- Screen reader output, and opening the `mailto:` link in a mail client.
- Other browsers (Firefox, Safari); Chromium only.
- Tablet slices for About, Leadership and Contact were generated but not individually viewed; the tablet full-page captures were generated but only the section slices (hero, academics, timeline, MUN, competitions, aviation, motorsport, quizzing, reading, law) were viewed.
- Night-block contrast was not measured by the script (background lookup failed); it was judged by eye.
