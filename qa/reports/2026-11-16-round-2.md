## Defects

D1. Major, phone 390 and 360, Model UN, Delegate awards record. The record table is squeezed into a column about 110px wide at the left of the 326px content width, with about 215px of empty space to its right (`round-2-sections/m-mun-4.png`, `mun-awards-390.png`). Conference names are set tight against the year ("SHISMUN2026" with no gap, "Eurivox MUN" wrapped to two lines, "High Commendation" wrapped to two lines), and the group heading's count wraps under the year ("2026" then "3 awards" on a second line). The DOM shows `table-layout: fixed` on `table.record` with `col` classes `rc-conf`, `rc-comm`, `rc-award`, `rc-year`; the table is 326px wide, so the cells are stacked inside a narrow first column. This is new since round 1b (where the phone sort sheet looked correct) and comes from the fixed-layout change in item 9. At 834 and 1440 the same table is correct (`mun-awards-834.png`).

D2. Minor, phone 390 and 360, Model UN, committee roll. Each committee name is a 42px-high button, 22 to 81px wide (FIA 22px, CCC 30px, AAIB 32px). The review set 44px of hit area through padding; the height is 2px short and the widths are well under 44 (WCAG 2.5.8 AA is met by spacing, 2.5.5 AAA and the brief's 44px target is not).

D3. Minor, phone and tablet, Reading. The sticky "400+" in the right column stays visible beside the marks and, on the first screen of the section, appears under the large "400+" numeral and sentence (`m-reading-1.png`; at tablet it floats alone at the right edge mid-screen, `t-reading-2.png`). The creative director listed this as deferred; it is still there.

D4. Minor, desktop, Timeline, last entry. With the last entry ("IIT Madras: Introduction to Law") on the reading mark, the outbound line (vertical stub at x 706) runs straight through the panel's title and body text ("Introduction to Law", "Certificate for ... project") (`tl-entry-23.png`). Legibility holds (hairline) but a line through the title at the one state the user dwells on is a visible flaw.

D5. Minor, desktop, Timeline, first 25px of scroll after pin start. When the page is at the pin start plus the first entry's offset (reached by Tab, scroll 4079), the vertical line sits at x 186 and passes through the "2013 - 2026" chapter note (`tl-entry-first.png`). At pin start (x 210) it is clear (`d-timeline-1.png`). Transient.

D6. Minor, desktop, Leadership, date ticks. The 12px tick for the two-line date "Elected 2025, re-elected 2026" and "March - April 2026" is at a fractional pixel y, so it renders as a faint grey dash beside the orange single-line ticks (`leadership-ticks.png`; computed colour is the Line colour at opacity 1, so this is anti-aliasing). The Student Coordinator title ("commerce competitions") ends 17px before the points column (`d-leadership-1.png`), tight.

D7. Minor, phone 390, Motorsport and Aviation. "Sector 2" sits about 7px from the inner edge of the track (review asked 16px) (`m-motorsport-1.png`); one waypoint triangle on the phone flight path sits on the line (`m-aviation-1.png`, x 360, y 470).

D8. Minor (cosmetic), reduced motion, hero. The end dot at the bottom of the hero line stays at opacity 0, so with reduced motion the line simply runs on with no terminal dot. Nothing is hidden that carries content. It reads as a harmless difference, but the review asked for the dot to appear.

Carried over from round 1 and not re-checked: page title carries only the name (client copy decision); Rasmanjari description still reads "Rasmanjari is described as his school's flagship interschool competition ..." (content flagged as unconfirmed; the text is visible in `d-leadership-2.png`); timeline layout shift (engineer skipped item 13's layout-shift fix; round 1 measured CLS 1.99 and it has not been re-measured).

## Design judgement

Academic profile and Model UN now read as art-directed (numerals set against ruled lists on the line, one column edge for the geopolitics block, group rows that act as breaks in the record), and Leadership is clearly better: the date and tick on the meridian make it a log along the line rather than a resume table, although it is still a role / points / logo list below the date column. Still weak: the phone MUN awards table (broken, D1), the "Courses and certifications" and "Further competitions" tables (single flat list, Competitions desktop is long at 4,196px), and the placeholder line, which is clear and consistent but at 12 repeats begins to read as a template rather than a deliberate gap.
