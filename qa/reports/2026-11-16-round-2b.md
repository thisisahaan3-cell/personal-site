**D6 (minor, leadership ticks): PARTIAL (still minor).**
All six ticks are the same colour (232,69,30), opacity 1, 12 x 1.5px. At device scale 1 every tick renders as 2 solid pixel rows. On high-DPI screens the 1.5px height snaps differently depending on position: at 2x, ticks 1, 4 and 5 (the "Elected 2025, re-elected 2026", "Rasmanjari" and the later two-line date rows) render 2 device rows (1 CSS px) while ticks 0, 2 and 3 render 4 rows; at 3x it is 3 versus 6 rows. So on a retina display some ticks are half the weight of others (`tk-2x-*.png`, `tk-3x-*.png`, `leadership-ticks.png`). The previous faint grey is gone (colour is now correct). Fix: use a whole-pixel tick height (1px or 2px), not 1.5px. The Student Coordinator title gap was not re-measured.
