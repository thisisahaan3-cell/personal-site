## 5. Global 3D rules
- Loading: Three.js is dynamically imported per route after first paint. Home stays raw WebGL.
- Colour:
  - `SRGBColorSpace` output and AgX tone mapping.
  - Colour and emissive textures in sRGB; everything else linear.
- HDRI: 1k through PMREM, as `scene.environment` only. The background is always the page ground.
- Shadows: one PCFSoft key light (2048 desktop, 1024 phone), plus baked contact-shadow planes.
- Assets:
  - GLBs meshopt-compressed with KTX2 textures at 1k (2k for the bull).
  - `InstancedMesh` for furniture.
  - Budget: 6 MB per page on desktop, 2.5 MB on phone.
- Rendering:
  - DPR capped at 1.75 on desktop and 1.5 on phone.
  - Pause off screen or when the tab is hidden; render on demand when idle.
  - No bloom or post-processing.
- Fallbacks:
  - Phones and devices with `deviceMemory` ≤ 4: baked shadows, no light cookie.
  - No WebGL2, save-data or a failed load: `public/stills/<page>.webp`, rendered from the same scene, with the hotspots as a DOM list.
  - Reduced motion: one settled frame, no camera moves, no torch; selection still works with instant cuts.
- Hotspots: real `<button>`s in reading order, positioned by projection. Focusing one moves the camera to it; every fact is also in the DOM record.
- Credits: CC-BY models get a credit line in the page foot.
