## 2. Rulings

### 2.1 The engineer's ten deviations

1. **Arrow in Geist in "2,000 → 100": rejected.** Section 1.2 already says no arrows anywhere. The cell reads "100 of 2,000", all in Instrument Serif, the same wording the figure's crosshair uses. The content researcher changes the stat's display value; the engineer does not compose it.
2. **Phone finance scale ends at x = 288: accepted.** The geometry in 3.6 was wrong. System amended.
3. **2022 Scholar Badge ring on the axis-break mark: rejected.** A year covered by the Scholar Badge span counts as a year with a mark. 2022 gets its own bay with zero entries (64 wide by the existing formula), a year tick, the year label and the ring. The break mark is only for years with nothing at all.
4. **Detail panel fades when "In progress" arrives: accepted, with a condition.** The last entry must show its full panel while it rests on the reading mark. The exchange is specified in fix 13.
5. **Artha phone screenshot overlapping from inside: accepted** as built. System amended.
6. **First Night chapter's top space is 2x the chapter gap with the 240 included: accepted.** More empty night is not wanted. The last Night chapter's bottom space also drops to 1x the chapter gap (fix 14).
7. **Fellowship labels right of the raised step: accepted.** It reads better than the reference.
8. **Reading counts right-aligned to x = 108 with shortened marks: rejected on desktop and tablet, accepted on phone.** The labels touch the mark ends and crowd the "400+" numeral. Replacement in fix 12.
9. **Portrait settles at scale 1.06: accepted.** 3.0 already says so; 5.1 follows it.
10. **Phone circuit drawn by the engineer: accepted.** It does not resemble a real circuit and reads as the same lap simplified. It must carry all three sector labels (fix 14).

### 2.2 Line ink (section 7 against 8.1)

**8.1 wins, with one exception.** The orange is the line. Orange words elsewhere spend the one bold thing on a footer link.

- `line-ink` (#B8330F) is used in exactly one place: the title of the entry currently selected on the horizontal timeline. That text names a mark on the line, and the state is also carried by the filled dot and the thicker stem.
- Inline links are navy with a 1px navy underline; hover wipes in a 1.5px navy underline. This applies to "Back to top", the quiz source link and the email link in the Contents overlay.
- `line` and `line-night` stay for the line and its marks only. No orange underlines, buttons, icons or hover states.

Sections 1.1, 2.7, 8.1 and 8.2 of the design system are edited to say this.

### 2.3 Placeholders

Twelve identical blocks is a rhythm problem, not a content problem. Every slot stays visible, labelled and editable (`placeholder: true` in the content file is untouched); only the amount of empty paper changes. Nobody writes his words for him.

Three forms:

- **Full block** (label line and three hairline rules): once on the page, for "Biography and personal statement" in About.
- **Document form**: paragraph 2 of the Law document, as built, with six empty ruled lines after the label and no more.
- **Placeholder line** (new, the default): one row 44px high. Slot name at the left and "In Ahaan's words, to come" at the right, both `data` slate (night-slate on night), on one baseline, with a single hairline rule at the bottom of the row. Width: its column, at most 34rem. No blank rules beneath. On phone, if the two strings do not fit on one line the phrase drops to a second line, left-aligned; neither string wraps inside itself. It is always a standalone row 32px below the block before it, never a row inside a list headed "From the resume".
