## 3D models and HDRIs

Retrieved 17 Nov 2026 from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) through its public API (https://api.polyhaven.com), no login. All assets are **CC0** (public domain; no attribution required, the author is credited here anyway). Each model was downloaded as the 1k glTF with its textures, then converted by `scripts/fetch-polyhaven.mjs` to one GLB with `gltf-transform optimize --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024 --compress meshopt`. The GLBs therefore need **meshopt decoding** (three.js `MeshoptDecoder`) and WebP texture support (every current browser). Each parses with `scripts/inspect-models.mjs` (and `npx @gltf-transform/cli inspect`). Dimensions are the bounding box of the default scene in metres (glTF units) as W x H x D, for scaling. Manifest of raw facts: `scripts/polyhaven-manifest.json`. These are atmosphere/props only; never captioned as Ahaan's own or as any event.
